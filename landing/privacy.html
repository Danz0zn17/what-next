Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  What Next is operated by Greenberries (Pty) Ltd, South Africa

This policy explains what personal information What Next collects through this website and the What Next service, why we collect it, who processes it for us, and how you can exercise your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). It should be read with our Terms & Conditions.

1. Who Is Responsible

Greenberries (Pty) Ltd is the responsible party for personal information processed through What Next. For any privacy question or request, email hello@greenberries.co.za.

2. What We Collect

When you visit this website

  • Usage analytics - pages viewed, referring site, and general device and browser information, collected with Plausible and PostHog. Both are configured on this site without cookies, and PostHog is set to record page views only (no click tracking and no session recording).
  • Error reports - if a page fails to load or a script breaks, Sentry receives a technical report (the error, the page address and browser details) so we can fix it.
  • Hosting logs - like any website, our host Netlify processes your IP address and request details to serve the site.

When you sign up for beta access

  • Your name and email address, and the optional "tools" and "use case" answers if you fill them in.

When you use the What Next service

  • Session summaries, project context, facts and git commit metadata that you or your AI assistant choose to save. This is stored locally on your machine by default and only syncs to our cloud database if you set an API key. See section 3 of our Terms & Conditions for details.
  • What Next does not store or transmit your project code, file contents, secrets or credentials.

3. Why We Use It

  • To create your beta account and API key, and email you about your access and product updates.
  • To provide the What Next service, including cloud sync if you turn it on.
  • To understand how the website is used and to find and fix errors.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who Processes It for Us

We use the following service providers (operators) to run What Next, and share personal information with them only for the purposes above:

  • Netlify - website hosting and beta sign-up form submissions.
  • Railway - cloud hosting for the What Next API and cloud database.
  • Resend - sending the beta welcome email.
  • Plausible and PostHog - website analytics.
  • Sentry - error monitoring.

Some of these providers store data outside South Africa, so your personal information may be processed in other countries.

5. Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies. Our analytics are configured not to set cookies.

6. How Long We Keep It

  • Beta sign-up details - kept while your beta access is active, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Cloud-synced service data - kept while your account is active, or until you ask us to delete it. We delete cloud records within 7 business days of a verified request.
  • Local data - stays on your machine under ~/.whatnext/ and is under your control. Deleting that folder removes it.

7. Your Rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct or delete personal information that is inaccurate, out of date or no longer needed.
  • Object to us processing your personal information, and ask us to stop sending you product update emails at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@greenberries.co.za from the address you signed up with, or include your API key for cloud data requests.

8. Security

This website is served only over HTTPS, and access to the What Next cloud API requires your API key. Keep your API key private, and do not save secrets or confidential client information in What Next.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as What Next changes. When we do, we will update the date above, and we will email beta users about material changes.

10. Contact
