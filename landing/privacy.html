Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026  ·  What Next is operated by Greenberries, Durban, South Africa

This page explains what data What Next (the tool) and whatnextai.co.za (this website) handle, where it is kept and how to remove it. It sits alongside our Terms & Conditions, which cover use of the tool.

1. The tool: your memory stays on your machine

What Next stores what you and your AI assistants choose to save - session summaries, decisions, next steps, facts, project intelligence (stack, folders, conventions), entity pages, feedback on facts, and git commit metadata (messages, times and changed file names, never file contents). By default all of it lives in a local SQLite database under ~/.whatnext/data/ and in context cards under ~/.whatnext/agents/ on your own computer.

What Next never reads or uploads your source code, file contents, passwords or credentials. Text that looks like a secret or an instruction aimed at an AI is neutralised or flagged when it is saved.

2. Optional cloud sync

If you set an API key (WHATNEXT_API_KEY), your saved memory is copied in the background to a Postgres database we run on Railway, so your other machines and tools can read it. Without a key nothing leaves your computer. Remove the key to stop syncing.

3. This website

  • Beta sign-up form - the name, email and optional details you enter are stored by Netlify Forms (our host) and used only to contact you about beta access and product updates.
  • Page views - we may count page views with PostHog. It is set up without cookies or local storage, without click autocapture and without session recording, so it cannot follow you between visits.
  • Page load timing - Cloudflare, which serves this site, measures how fast pages load (Cloudflare Web Analytics). It sets no cookies and does not identify you.
  • Error reports - if a script on this site breaks, Sentry may receive the error, the page address and your browser type, so we can fix it.
  • Fonts - fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the request.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not sell or rent your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who processes data for us

Netlify (website hosting and forms), Cloudflare (DNS, delivery and page load timing), Railway (cloud sync database), PostHog (page view counts), Sentry (error reports) and Google Fonts. Some of these run outside South Africa; we use them only to operate What Next.

5. Your rights under POPIA

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email hello@greenberries.co.za.

  • Local data - delete the ~/.whatnext/ folder on your machine.
  • Cloud data - email us with your API key or sign-up email and we will delete your cloud records within 7 business days.
  • Beta sign-up - email us from the address you signed up with and we will remove it.

You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).

6. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the effective date above and tell beta users of material changes by email.
